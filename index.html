How it works

Umami-MRNN combines two networks. A multilayer perceptron reads 1,093 sequence descriptors: amino-acid composition, dipeptide composition, dipeptide deviation from expected mean, and composition–transition–distribution features. A recurrent network reads the sequence residue by residue, each encoded by overlapping physicochemical properties. Their threshold estimates are averaged (weights 0.67 and 0.33). Peptides with a predicted threshold below 40 mmol/L are classed as umami.
